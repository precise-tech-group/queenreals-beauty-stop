Her Journey

From Kansas City to Vinton

A story of resilience, craft, and starting over — more than once.

Crishion Canaday is a dedicated entrepreneur with a passion for cosmetology and a deep commitment to her community. Born and raised in Kansas City, Missouri, Crishion began her career journey in business management before transitioning to the medical field. After relocating to Phoenix, Arizona, and focusing on raising her son, Crishion reignited her entrepreneurial dreams in 2019, launching a work-from-home business and becoming a self-employed hairstylist.

Despite facing personal challenges, including a hit-and-run accident and her son's behavioral struggles, Crishion remained focused on her goals. She returned to cosmetology school in 2022 and worked closely with a salon owner to refine her skills. Today, she serves clients in Vinton, Virginia, offering personalized hair services from home and helping individuals find the best products and styles for their unique needs.

Crishion's journey reflects resilience, perseverance, and a commitment to making a positive impact through her craft and business. With a deep passion for hairstyling and a drive to continually grow, she is dedicated to helping clients look and feel their best.
